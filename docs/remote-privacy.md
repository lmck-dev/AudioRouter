# Audio Router Remote - privacy policy

*Last updated 6 October 2026*

Audio Router Remote is an Android app by LMCK.DEV that controls Audio Router, a
desktop app running on your own Linux PC.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
reporting, and it sends nothing to LMCK.DEV or to anyone else.

## What the app stores on your phone

The pairing details for your PC: its network addresses, its port, and the
access token from the QR code you scanned. They stay on the phone until you
tap Unpair or uninstall the app.

## What travels over your network

The app talks only to the PC you paired it with. It reads your mixer's state
(channel names, fader positions, effects, the names of apps playing sound, and
sound levels for the meters) and sends the changes you make. This traffic
stays between your phone and your PC. It is not encrypted (plain HTTP), which is
why Audio Router accepts phones on your home network only, unless you
choose otherwise on the PC.

## Camera

The camera is used only to read the pairing QR code. The picture is read on the
phone and is never saved or sent anywhere.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children.
